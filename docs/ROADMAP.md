# Product Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Student foundation

- Onboarding: name, grade, and interface language
- Home screen and daily mission entry point
- Mission player for vocabulary, story, grammar, and listening
- Local progress and downloaded content
- RTL/LTR accessibility validation

## Milestone 2 — Grade 7 pilot content

- Analyze the authorized Grade 7 textbook source
- Build the curriculum matrix
- Produce the first reviewed story arc
- Add audio and age-appropriate exercises
- Pilot with a small student group

## Milestone 3 — Learning loop

- Spaced vocabulary review
- Skill mastery and gentle recovery after missed days
- Student progress screen
- Offline content versioning and migrations

## Later, not in the current scope

- Accounts and cloud synchronization
- Parent and teacher roles
- Messaging or teacher matching
- Automated pronunciation scoring

These items require separate safety, privacy, and product reviews before implementation.
